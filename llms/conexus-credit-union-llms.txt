# Conexus Credit Union

> Conexus Credit Union is Saskatchewan's largest credit union, a member-owned cooperative financial institution headquartered in Regina with over $10 billion in consolidated assets, more than 140,000 members, and roughly 30 branches across the province. Conexus does not publish a first-party public developer API or developer portal — consumer financial-data access is aggregator-mediated (notably Plaid in Canada) under Canada's not-yet-operational Consumer-Driven Banking (open banking) framework.

## Developer surface

- No first-party public API: developer.conexus.ca does not resolve, and /developer and /api on the corporate site return 404.
- No /.well-known/ discovery documents (security.txt, OIDC, OAuth, api-catalog, ai-plugin all 404).
- Financial-data access for members is provided through third-party aggregators (e.g. Plaid) via connection-based data sharing, not a first-party API.
- Canada's federal Consumer-Driven Banking (open banking) framework, overseen by the FCAC, is legislated but not yet operational; open-finance access remains voluntary and aggregator-mediated.
